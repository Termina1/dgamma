## Definition-60 faithfulness and strengthening judgment

### Equation 54 coverage

For the finite-list calculus, no recovery-relevant generator remains hidden:

- A first actual `LAdvance` supplies the anchor required by `IteratorStage`.
- `ReachableSuffix` supplies the current stage and every later nonempty static suffix, even if that suffix never executes in the trace (confirmed by the later-continuation probe).
- `IteratorForwardGenerator` supplies `pr1 . i'` for every such stage.
- `IteratorYieldedGenerator stage origin` supplies the individual inverse yielded by that stage at every full-effect origin where it succeeds; an undefined origin contributes the nowhere-defined partial map, which is harmless under the partial-map semantics.
- `TraceCompose` closes generators across origins and across stages, while `TraceIdentity` supplies the monoid identity. There is no missing “inverse of a continuation”: a continuation is another stage, whose own per-origin yielded inverses are generated in turn.

This matches paper Equation 54 within the explicitly finite/static representation. It does not represent the paper's data-dependent/coinductive continuation relation or registering iterations; README/NOTES already mark those as restrictions.

### Equation 55 coverage

`generatedMonoidsCommute` has the paper's full monoid-by-monoid quantification, not merely generator pairs or the endpoint accumulator. `iteratorYieldsStable` quantifies both actor directions through universally quantified `left`/`right`, every reachable stage, every foreign generated transformation, and every origin. `IteratorYieldAgreement` rejects mixed defined/undefined outcomes and compares the yielded inverse extensionally plus the continuation. The origin-shift probe confirms this field is independent of, and stronger than, mere map commutation in exactly the way Equation 55 requires.

### Non-blocking strengthening/deviation

There are two conservative differences from the literal paper:

1. `EffectStateRelated` is exact on ambient state and every table lookup rather than the paper's observational `≃`. This is already documented in `NOTES.md:488-493`.
2. `ActualForwardGenerator` (`src/DGamma/Metatheory.idr:1286-1311`) includes every actual action, whereas paper `M(i)` is generated by iterator forwards/inverses and gives orchestration rules `Ψ=id`. Most extras are identity. The implementation's O-Insert/O-Remove maps clear the actor table (`src/DGamma/Metatheory.idr:1042-1048`), so on arbitrary off-trace `EffectState`s these two extra generators are not literally paper identity. This can conservatively reject a paper-independent trace, though on reachable insert/remove states absent and empty tables coincide and no counterexample to the recovery statements results.

Neither difference makes Theorem 61, Corollary 62, or Theorem 64 unprovable as conditional statements. The positive two-actor and effectful witnesses demonstrate that the repaired premise is inhabitable. I therefore judge the finite encoding **slightly stronger than the paper, but not over-strengthened into vacuity or a proof blocker**.
