## Owner-decision memo: root normal form versus guard semantics

### Option 1 — revise the canonical placement clause, retain current guards

Replace unconditional root-before-every-lifecycle placement with a causal,
resource-compatible placement: a root insertion goes only as early as its
declared provisions are free, respecting prior live provision intervals,
registration licensing, required provider activation, and external input order.
For R174, root 2 must remain after removal of child 1 under the current guard.
This preserves the current executable trace language and its pairwise declared
provision invariant, and permits legitimate service re-provision after disposal.
It changes the paper-facing normal form and needs a new existence/uniqueness
argument and decreasing measure; “earliest feasible” is a direction, not an
already precise or proved replacement theorem. O19's dependent-consumer safety
issue remains separately outstanding. No code or theorem surface is revised.

### Option 2 — revise the model's service-ownership/guard semantics

Model actual provided service occupancy and its disposal boundary rather than
reserving every declared provision at fiber insertion. This may better match
the runtime, but changes Table-1 applicability and registry invariants, with
substantial preservation/resolution/coherence/replay proof obligations. It is
NOT the one-line rule “ignore any retired fiber”: a retired/disposed fiber can
still have an installed service while asynchronous cleanup is pending.

`c594d1aa4e901992f8836f9d747d5a640c9b9d58` (2026-09-06; not asserted to be the
