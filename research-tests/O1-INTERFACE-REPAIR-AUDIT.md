Branch baseline: `cp5-thm73-scoping@c01128eef1e4bf59b479ac87017d7d2721078f53`

Scope: grind shift #9 (overall #63). This is an audit artifact only. It changes
no frozen declaration, hole, package input, or production source.

## Result

**BLOCKED: the proposed loose transfer capital is not producer-suppliable in the
accepted pipeline, and the generic composition result has no consumer.**

The consumer/producer inventory below shows that the accepted O18–O22 path does
not produce two values of the generic premise package used by
`composeModuloVestigialEndpointSpike`. It instead carries four heterogeneous,
authenticated endpoint steps and directly consumes them in O21. Adding loose
forward/reverse transfer and root-presence fields to the raw pairwise relation
would therefore fail both halves of the anti-oscillation rule:

1. the fields are not needed by any current consumer of the generic output; and
2. no accepted producer can populate them for all generic pairwise cases.

Consequently no positive Idris probe can honestly construct the proposed
capital from every genuine producer. Per the interface-gate condition, the
repair stops here rather than introducing an uninhabited wrapper.

## 5. Sealed-composition alternative

The audit supports sealing, but **not** by adding another generic pairwise
wrapper. The genuine chain is heterogeneous:

```text
original-left
  -- CanonicalEndpointRelation --> canonical-left
  -- RelationalReplayEndpoint --> replayed-left
  -- ReplayedCanonicalEndpointBridge --> canonical-right
  -- inverse CanonicalEndpointRelation --> original-right
```

The outer generation/current authority comes once from
`SameOrchestrationModuloGenerated`; exact deleted-generation membership comes
from `AcceptedDeletionScannerCapital`. These are already precisely the inputs of
`replayedCanonicalToOriginalEndpointSpike`.

Recommended decomposition:

1. Treat `replayedCanonicalToOriginalEndpointSpike` as the sole sealed endpoint
   composition theorem for the accepted confluence pipeline.
2. Prove its final four-way controls directly by scanner/canonical-endpoint
   induction, using the exact replay and bridge steps only for the non-vestigial
   middle.
3. Retain the already-proved pure algebra (`composeGenerationBijection`,
   `composeNameBijection`, and `composeRegistrationCorrespondence`) as private or
   separately documented helpers where genuinely useful.
4. Remove the claim that arbitrary
   `SystemEquivalentByRenamingModuloVestigial` is transitive, or replace O1 only
   after an explicit decision with a true restricted statement that has a real
   consumer and producer.

This avoids caller-selected transfer maps and derives each needed invariant once
at its actual source. It also avoids adding redundant capital to the public CP3
records, preserving the production blob.

## 6. Blocker and required decision

**BLOCKER:** the gate asked for positive producer probes before repair, but the
accepted pipeline contains no producer of the raw pairwise endpoint-relation
premises and no consumer of their generic composed result. Therefore the
proposed repair cannot satisfy the anti-oscillation rule.

A new decomposition decision is required before declarations change:

- retire/reclassify the orphan generic O1 theorem and move the remaining endpoint
  proof work wholly under O21's existing sealed boundary; or
- identify a concrete accepted producer/consumer pair not present in the current
  26-hole plan and authorize its exact interface.
