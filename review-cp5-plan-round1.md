## Consolidated answers

### Obligation completeness

The list is **not sufficient** as written. Deletion iteration does at least package alignment, discipline, initial well-formed/empty, quiet, no-failure, totality, and independence in `CanonicalizationPremises`, and the same generic construction could be invoked symmetrically for left and right. But it loses same-external-input and cumulative registration evidence; does not rebuild all Lemma-68/70 rank/reachability premises explicitly; does not transport the original-endpoint support indices needed by `CanonicalSchedule`; and has no full recursive invariant package for sorting. The final outer constructor has the right exact type, but the O21 bridge preceding it is spiked with the wrong relation families.

### Spike statement shape

All five research modules elaborate, but elaboration is not adequacy. The deletion result is too weak for the schedule, the local diamond omits tags/outcomes, the sorting result is indexed at the reduced endpoint, the mapped order omits transported comparability, cross-trace convergence omits independence, and renaming composition does not accept the canonical-reduction records actually produced. `traceIndependentAfterDeletionReplaySpike` has the right round-N output trace index, but its universal quantification is inconsistent with the proposed fallback of adding evidence only to an internal D72 result.

### Estimate sanity

The 24-shift floor is not credible and 34 is not an honest recommendation for 18 large-or-harder obligations, especially after adding generic replay independence and O/O swaps. Until corrected interfaces elaborate, plan approximately **50–90 shifts**, with O2, O5, O12, O16, and O21 each potentially Lemma-72-scale or larger.

### Decision points

Keep the already resolved rich `SameOrchestrationModuloGenerated` premise. Convert activation-tag scope, maximality, vestigial closure, and root placement from “decisions” into prove/refute gates. Add actual decisions for Path-A abandonment versus direct canonical evaluation, adoption of enriched internal reduction/schedule witnesses, escalation if original↔reduced support transport fails, and algorithm redesign if O/O transposition fails.

## Verdict

**REJECT.** The release tree and research isolation are clean, every spike elaborates, and the outer `ConfluenceResult` assembly is exact; however, multiple spike interfaces are strictly weaker than the evidence needed to inhabit immutable `confluenceTheorem`, one cross-trace spike omits the premise that makes its conclusion true, and crucial replay/support/O-O/composition obligations are absent. These are scoping defects, not implementation details, so the plan must incorporate all twelve changes above and undergo another adversarial plan review before proof shifts are authorized.
